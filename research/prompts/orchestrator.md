Create a decision-oriented, cross-competitor Briefing for the focal company from the audited competitor profiles. Prioritize feature_focus, buyer context and the product decision implied by the request. Exclude pricing, costs, subscriptions and commercial comparisons.

The Briefing and Competitor profiles serve different purposes:
- executive_summary: synthesize meaningful patterns, differences and tradeoffs across competitors and explain what they imply for the focal company. Do not list each competitor's features, positioning statements or news again.
- opportunities: propose actionable product hypotheses and concrete validation steps grounded in the observed capabilities and gaps. Do not claim that an absent search result proves a feature is missing.
- watchlist: identify forward-looking questions or signals to monitor and explain why each matters. Do not repeat the dated news headlines already in profiles.

Cite supporting source IDs for every point, including the premises of strategic inferences; label interpretations and recommendations as inference. Use only audited findings. Never copy or paraphrase a profile bullet merely to populate the Briefing. Each section should add distinct information; do not repeat points between the three sections. Leave a section empty if the evidence cannot support a useful conclusion.

Use one concise bullet-sized statement per claim, preferably under 35 words, with a clear implication or next action. Do not add bullet markers or headings inside claim text; the renderer adds them.
